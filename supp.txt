                               I.1    Ensuring variance identification
                               Asafirststepinpost-processing,wecheckforeachdrawδr fromtheCFAmodelwhether
                               the 3579 counting rule is satisfied and the variance decomposition is unique. The efÏcient
                               algorithm of Hosszejni and Fruh¨ wirth-Schnatter (2022) for the verification of the 3579
                               counting rule is used for this purpose. It is implemented in R and MATLAB and the
                               computercodeispubliclyavailable at https://github.com/hdarjus/sparvarideand,
                               respectively, https://github.com/hdarjus/sparvaride-matlab.
                                   In our experience, the fraction p      of variance identified draws is relatively high for
                                                                       V
                               reasonably chosen priors. This is documented in full detail in Appendix K.3 for the var-
                               ious simulation scenarios considered in Section 5.1. A low fraction of variance identified
                               draws can be the result of poorly chosen shrinkage priors and is a hint to consider an-
                               other prior family. Using a fractional prior as slab distribution for the loadings β , for
                                                                                                                          ij
                               instance, tends to deliver a low fraction of variance identified draws in high-dimensional
                               factor models, see Section 5.3 for illustration.

                           I.3   Resolving rotational invariance
                           Due to the UGLT structure imposed on βr, rotational invariance reduces for variance

                                                 r                 r      1      r

                            t

                                                                  1      r

                                     Appendix J: MCMC for sparse BFA under a PLT
                                                                 condition
                                     A minor modification of Algorithm 1 allows us to perform sparse BFA under a PLT
                                     condition for a fixed number r of factors. Due to the PLT condition the r pivots occupy
                                     the first r rows, i.e. (l ,...,l ) = (1,...,r). As under the UGLT condition, the loadings
                                                                   1        r
                                     below the pivot rows are subject to variable selection. Priors are the same as under
                                     UGLT, however the maximum number of factors is equal to H = r.
                                          Algorithm 1 is started with the selected number of factors r, no spurious columns
                                     and l      = (1,...,r). Both r and l are held fixed throughout all MCMC iterations
                                             r                                        r
                                     and spurious columns that might pop up during variable selection in Step (D) are not
                                     removed. This modified sampler skips Step (D-b) and Step (L) in the (CFA) part as
                                     well as the entire (EFA) part and consists of the following steps: (H), (D-a), (P), (F),
                                     (S) and (A).
                                          All posterior draws exhibit k = r non-zero columns some of which might be spurious.
                                     Due to the PLT condition, rotational invariance is not an issue and no reordering of
                                     the columns is performed. However, posterior draws that are not variance identified are
                                     removed.
